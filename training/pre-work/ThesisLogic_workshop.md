## Preparing your final presentation

Use these answers to build Friday’s presentation around **one core question, a focused method and a result**. Explain the model generally, then focus on the particular feature needed to understand your result and its economic interpretation.

If your work remains incomplete, present the method, diagnostic or implementation obstacle, what you tested and what you learned. Keep expected results distinct from observed findings.

The presentation is **10 minutes followed by 5 minutes of discussion**, with a **maximum of eight slides including the title slide**. A literature review, a contribution section and a complete model description are unnecessary for this workshop presentation. Group facilitators may present if time permits.

This document is a guide to clarifying your research logic and choosing a feasible workshop task. Revise your answers as the work develops; wider thesis context can remain in your thesis without needing to appear in this focused exercise or presentation.
